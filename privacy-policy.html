Conscribe — Privacy Policy

Last updated August 31, 2026

Conscribe has no accounts and no user database. Your lists live on your devices. When they sync, they are encrypted end to end, and the server that passes them along cannot read them.

What is collected

Nothing that identifies you. There is no sign-up, no account, no email address, no phone number, and no profile. Nobody operating Conscribe has a list of who uses it, because no such list is created.

Your lists

Your lists are stored on your device. When you share a list with other devices in your household, they sync directly over your local network when those devices are together, and through a relay server when they are apart. In both cases the contents are encrypted on the sending device and decrypted on the receiving one.

The relay

The relay holds encrypted messages until the other device collects them. It is designed to know as little as possible, and what it can see is worth stating plainly:

  • It cannot read your lists. It receives bytes it has no key for.
  • Messages are addressed to identifiers derived from keys the relay does not have and cannot compute. The ones your devices use for routine syncing change every hour. The one used to hand a new device its invitation lasts as long as that invitation does, which is at most a day.
  • It does see the network address a message arrives from and the time it arrives, as any server does.
  • Messages are deleted after seven days. Larger transfers are deleted after ninety.

If you would rather not use the default relay, you can point the app at a different one, including one you run yourself.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications, a separate small service asks Apple or Google to wake your other device. That service receives a device token, the time, and one flag saying whether the notification should be visible or silent — which means it can tell that something was added to a list, but not to which list, by whom, or what it was. It never receives your lists, your household, or anything the relay holds. It is deliberately a different program from the relay so that neither one holds both halves.

Diagnostics

The app keeps a diagnostic log on your device to help explain failures. It stays there. It is sent only if you choose to send it, and it is encrypted before it leaves your device. Nothing is uploaded on its own.

Permissions

  • Camera — only to scan a pairing code when adding a device. Images are not stored and not transmitted.
  • Local network — to find your other devices so they can sync directly instead of through the relay.
  • Notifications — optional, and only asked for when you turn them on.

Conscribe does not ask for your location, contacts, or photo library.

No analytics, advertising, or tracking

There are no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no third-party software development kits. Conscribe does not contact any service other than the relay you have configured and, if you have enabled notifications, the wake service.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. There is no account to close and no copy held anywhere to request the deletion of. Anything still sitting on the relay expires on the schedule above.

Children

Conscribe is not directed at children, and collects no personal information from anyone regardless of age.
