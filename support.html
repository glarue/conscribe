Conscribe — Support

Last updated November 5, 2026

Questions, problems or ideas: contact@conscribeapp.com

Common questions

How do I add another device? On a device that already has your list, open Settings, then Household, then Add a device. Scan the QR code with the new device, or send it the invite link.

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up. Your devices recognize each other through the invite.

What happens when the free trial ends? Nothing is deleted. Every device keeps its lists and can still edit and export them. Syncing between devices and sharing lists pause until someone in the household unlocks Conscribe with a one-time purchase.

How do I restore the Unlock? If another device in your household bought it, the whole household is already unlocked. If you reinstalled the app or started a new household, open Settings, then Unlock, then Restore purchase, signed in to the same Apple or Google account you bought it with.

Something didn't sync. Open Settings, then Advanced, then Upload log for support. The app encrypts the log and gives you a code; email us the code and what you saw. Only someone with the code can read the log.

Privacy

See the privacy policy.

Conscribe keeps your lists on your devices. The relay that syncs them stores only encrypted data it cannot read.
